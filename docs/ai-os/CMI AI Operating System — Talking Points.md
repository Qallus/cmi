# CMI AI Operating System — Talking Points

Oct 21, 2026 · @Jeremy Waters

## Big Picture

- Goal: a CMI "AI operating system" that connects our existing tools, not replaces them
- AI is the intelligence layer; the CMI web app holds the data
- Shift from planning to using: first tools in the team's hands in 1–2 weeks

## 1. Platform & Accounts

- Company Business/Enterprise plan (ChatGPT or Claude), one account per employee
- Replaces the single shared account: better permissions, security, and personal context
- Admin publishes shared tools once; everyone gets them
- Decision needed: ChatGPT Business or Claude Team/Enterprise

## 2. Individual Assistants & Daily Briefings

- Each person connects their own Gmail, Calendar, and Slack
- Morning briefing: meetings, follow-ups, emails needing a reply, project updates, deadlines
- Works on day one with built-in connectors — no custom build needed
- Ready now: quickest win to roll out first

## 3. Executive Briefing & Oversight

- Answers: unanswered client emails, open commitments, stale proposals, project delays, dropped items
- AI account alone can't do it — each connector sees only its owner's inbox
- CMI web app reads company email (one Google admin approval), tracks items over time
- Daily executive briefing plus weekly company review, with drill-down by project or person
- Disclose company-wide email monitoring to the team in a short policy

## 4. Centralized Knowledge Base

- One source: SOPs, templates, job info, cost history, roles — stored in the CMI web app
- Every AI account reads the same knowledge; update once, everyone gets it
- Not tied to anyone's personal AI chats or memory
- Permissions by role; leadership sees everything
- Foundation piece — build this first

## 5. Shared Agents, Forms & Workflows

- Workflows defined once, published to everyone, updated centrally
- Daily Log: pick project, dictate, add photos, AI formats it, saves to project, flags follow-ups
- Daily Log form lives in the CMI web app (photos go straight to project files)
- Next workflows: meeting summaries, weekly status, client drafts, sub follow-ups, closeout checklists

## 6. Integrations

- Start: Google Workspace (Gmail, Calendar, Drive) and Slack
- Later: Adaptive, Plaud, QuickBooks (about 1–2 weeks each)
- AI pulls from the source of truth; no duplicate copies
- Open question: does Adaptive offer API access?

## 7. Commitment & Action Tracking

- AI spots promises in email, Slack, and meetings ("budget by next week")
- Logs owner, project, due date; keeps it visible until done
- Closes items when a reply or project update shows it's handled
- Needs a database in the CMI web app, not just a chat connector

## 8. Scalability & Flexibility

- Knowledge, workflows, and records live in the CMI web app, not the AI provider
- Switching ChatGPT ↔ Claude later means rewiring a thin layer, not rebuilding
- Role-based permissions grow with the team

## 9. Rollout & Decisions

1. Week 1: set up accounts; everyone connects email, calendar, Slack; personal briefings start
2. Weeks 1–2: knowledge base and Daily Log in the CMI web app
3. About 2 weeks: first executive briefing (open commitments, unanswered client emails)
4. Weeks 3–4: status tracking, tuning on real data
5. After: Adaptive, Plaud, QuickBooks

PROMPT 2 — Constructed Matter Landing Page: ADU / Casita Addition

Create a new high-converting landing page for Constructed Matter, Inc. focused on ADUs, casitas, guest houses, and detached or attached secondary living spaces.

The page should educate homeowners on the value of adding an ADU or casita to their property. The messaging should show that an ADU can be useful for family, guests, aging parents, adult children, rental income, a private office, a studio, or a peaceful space away from the main house.

Build this as a premium, modern construction landing page that matches the Constructed Matter brand: clean, upscale, professional, architectural, warm, and trustworthy.

Core Page Goals:

* Encourage homeowners to schedule a consultation.
* Position Constructed Matter as a trusted design/build partner for ADUs and casitas.
* Explain the lifestyle and financial benefits of an ADU.
* Highlight family use, guest use, income potential, and private personal space.
* Make the page feel local, premium, and practical.

Page Sections to Create:

1. Hero Section
   Headline ideas:
   “Add More Living Space Without Leaving Home”
   or
   “Build a Casita That Adds Flexibility, Comfort, and Long-Term Value”

Subheadline:
Whether you need space for family, guests, rental income, a private office, or a retreat of your own, Constructed Matter helps homeowners design and build ADUs and casitas that feel intentional, beautiful, and connected to the property.

Primary CTA:
“Schedule an ADU Consultation”

Secondary CTA:
“Explore Casita Options”

Visual direction:
Use a premium casita, backyard guest house, or detached ADU image. The design should feel warm, modern, and residential.

2. What Is an ADU / Casita Section
   Title:
   “What Is an ADU or Casita?”

Copy:
An ADU, accessory dwelling unit, or casita is a secondary living space added to your property. It can be attached to the main home, detached in the backyard, or designed as a private guest house depending on the property, local requirements, and homeowner goals.

Keep the explanation simple and homeowner-friendly.

3. Benefits Section
   Title:
   “More Than Extra Space”

Create cards or feature blocks for:

* Space for family members
* Guest accommodations
* Aging parent or multi-generational living
* Adult children or extended family
* Rental or income potential
* Private home office
* Creative studio or gym
* Space away from the main house
* Long-term property value

4. Family Living Section
   Title:
   “Keep Family Close While Giving Everyone Their Own Space”

Copy should explain:
A casita can provide independence and privacy while keeping loved ones nearby. This can be ideal for aging parents, visiting family, adult children, caregivers, or long-term guests.

5. Income Potential Section
   Title:
   “Create a Space That Can Work for You”

Copy should explain:
Depending on local rules, property setup, and homeowner goals, an ADU may create opportunities for rental income, guest stays, or long-term flexibility. Avoid guaranteeing income. Use careful wording like “may,” “can,” and “potential.”

Include a note:
Rental use and ADU requirements vary by city, zoning, HOA, and property. Constructed Matter can help homeowners think through feasibility and planning considerations.

6. Private Retreat Section
   Title:
   “Your Space Away From the Main House”

Position the ADU as more than just a guest house:

* Private office
* Design studio
* Fitness space
* Music room
* Pool house
* Reading room
* Hobby space
* Quiet retreat

7. Design + Build Possibilities Section
   Title:
   “Designed Around Your Property and Lifestyle”

Include possible ADU/casita features:

* Bedroom and bathroom
* Kitchenette or full kitchen
* Living area
* Private entrance
* Patio or outdoor connection
* Storage
* Laundry
* Energy-efficient design
* Matching exterior finishes
* Modern interior finishes

8. Why Constructed Matter Section
   Position Constructed Matter as experienced, detail-oriented, design-forward, and organized.

Include points like:

* Thoughtful planning
* Design and construction experience
* Clear communication
* Quality craftsmanship
* Local project understanding
* Functional layouts
* Premium finishes
* Project management from concept to completion

9. Process Section
   Title:
   “From Idea to Feasibility to Build”

Steps:

1. Initial consultation

2. Property and goals review

3. Feasibility discussion

4. Design direction

5. Scope and budget planning

6. Permitting and preparation

7. Construction

8. Final walkthrough

9. CTA Section
   Title:
   “Thinking About Adding a Casita or ADU?”

Copy:
Let’s explore what is possible on your property and how an ADU could add flexibility, comfort, and long-term value.

Button:
“Schedule a Consultation”

Development Instructions:

* Use clean, responsive layout.
* Use modern cards, soft shadows, premium spacing, and subtle animations.
* Make the page mobile-first.
* Add placeholder image areas if actual images are not available.
* Make CTA buttons consistent with the Constructed Matter website.
* Include SEO-friendly headings.
* Use semantic HTML and accessible button/link labels.
* Keep the copy polished, professional, and easy for homeowners to understand.
* Avoid guaranteeing rental income, permitting approval, zoning approval, or property value increases.
* The page should feel premium, warm, trustworthy, and practical.
